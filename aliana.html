01 / Biography

Connecting biomedical research with regenerative innovation.

Associate Professor Dr. Nur Aliana Hidayah Mohamed is a Senior Lecturer at the Faculty of Dentistry, Universiti Teknologi MARA (UiTM). Her academic background encompasses biomedical sciences, pharmacology and tissue engineering, with particular interests in drug delivery systems and bone regeneration.

She holds a PhD in Drug Delivery and Tissue Regeneration from the University of Nottingham. Her research interests include drug delivery carriers, bone-regenerative materials and the development of MicroX, described as a bone graft material.

Her academic and teaching experience spans Malaysia, Japan, China, Italy and the United Kingdom. She also contributes to community initiatives involving rural education and mental health awareness.

In her role leading UiTM's Microindustrial Hub (Tissue Engineering), she is engaged in industry collaboration and product commercialisation. She is also involved in international scientific societies supporting medical science and education.
