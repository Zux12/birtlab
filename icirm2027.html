The conference

Where discovery meets clinical purpose.

Scientific programme

12 subthemes.
One clinical purpose.

Explore the focus areas for invited talks, oral presentations and posters.

01Clinical Evidence, Trials & Patient Outcomes+

Clinical studies, safety and effectiveness, outcome measures, long-term follow-up, and lessons from successful or unsuccessful translation.

02Wound Healing, Skin & Soft-Tissue Regeneration+

Chronic wounds, diabetic wounds, burns, skin substitutes, advanced dressings, and soft-tissue reconstruction.

03Orthopaedic & Musculoskeletal Regeneration+

Bone, cartilage, tendon, ligament, and muscle repair; joint degeneration; and functional recovery.

04Dental, Oral & Craniofacial Regeneration+

Periodontal regeneration, dental pulp, alveolar bone, oral tissues, and craniofacial reconstruction.

05Organ Repair & Functional Regeneration+

Regenerative approaches for cardiac, vascular, neural, ocular, and other organ systems, with clear identification of development stage.

06Stem Cells, Cell-Based & Cell-Free Therapies+

Stem cells, engineered cells, secretomes, extracellular vesicles, mechanisms of action, and evidence supporting therapeutic development.

07Biomaterials, Scaffolds & Regenerative Medical Devices+

Hydrogels, bioactive materials, scaffolds, implant interfaces, delivery systems, and assessment of biological and functional performance.

08Biofabrication, Bioprinting & Engineered Tissues+

Bioinks, three-dimensional bioprinting, vascularisation, tissue constructs, and fabrication approaches addressing clinical needs.

09Organoids, Disease Models & Preclinical Validation+

Organoids, organ-on-chip systems, disease modelling, therapeutic testing, and models that help assess readiness for clinical research.

10AI, Digital Technologies & Precision Regenerative Medicine+

Computational modelling, AI-assisted material and scaffold design, imaging, predictive analytics, and patient-specific approaches.

11Biomanufacturing, Quality & Regulatory Translation+

Manufacturing scale-up, reproducibility, quality control, good manufacturing practice, and pathways towards clinical evaluation.

12Clinical Adoption, Ethics & Commercialisation+

Clinical implementation, informed consent, responsible communication, affordability, technology transfer, and industry–hospital collaboration.
